Ergin DJ Lab

A high-end procedural DJ console that runs entirely in your browser.

Designed & Created by Ergin Kaplan
  • Two decks — independent Play/Pause, BPM and vibe presets.
  • Full mixer — 3-band EQ (Low/Mid/High), trim, gain and equal-power crossfader.
  • Live FX — Reverb, Delay and a performance Low-Pass / High-Pass filter per deck.
  • Real synthesis — kicks, snares, hats, bass, chords and leads generated from oscillators.
  • Visuals — spectrum analyser, oscilloscopes and VU meters reacting in real time.

All audio is procedurally generated in your browser using the Web Audio API. No copyrighted material or user uploads are used. 100% Copyright-Free.
